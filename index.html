About JIMS Greater Noida

A leading campus under the Jagan Nath Gupta Memorial Educational Society

Academic Credibility & Affiliation

JIMS Engineering Management Technical Campus (JEMTEC) is situated at Knowledge Park III in Greater Noida. The institute is affiliated to Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU), New Delhi and is approved by the AICTE and the Bar Council of India (BCI).

All degrees are awarded directly by GGSIPU, ensuring recognized curriculum standards, experienced faculty, and strong corporate value across India.

Why Choose JEMTEC?

✓ State University Degree: Direct degree from GGSIPU, Delhi.

✓ Modern Computing Labs: High-performance AI, IoT, and Cloud labs.

✓ Court Training: Approved Moot Court for law aspirants.

✓ Strategic Location: Close to Knowledge Park II Metro Station.
